How the calculation works

Price per standard unit = total pack price ÷ total quantity in standard units.

Litres: convert millilitres to litres by dividing by 1,000. Kilograms: convert grams to kilograms by dividing by 1,000. Then divide the pack price by pack count × quantity per item. Example: 3 × 750 ml bottles at €4.50 → 3 × 0.75 L = 2.25 L → €4.50 ÷ 2.25 L = €2.00/L.

Data and privacy

Your records are stored in this browser using IndexedDB. They are not sent to a server. Clearing browser data can erase them, so export a JSON backup regularly. The backup contains products, price records, supermarkets and tags.
